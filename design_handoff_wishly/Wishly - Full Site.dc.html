Wishly — the whole site, rebuilt

Same structure we settled on in 4a–4d, redrawn against a stricter system. Six changes did most of the work:

Paper, not tintLilac backgrounds everywhere read cheap at scale. Warm paper #FAF8F5 carries the pages; violet is now only ever an action.
One display faceInstrument Serif for headlines above 30px only, Gabarito for every piece of interface. Nothing else.
Decorative emoji removedThe 🙈 became a drawn eye-off mark. Emoji now appears in exactly one place: the list's own identity, which you pick.
Hairlines over shadowsOne shadow token, used on cards and nothing else. Coloured glows gone. Radii fixed at 10 / 14 / 999.
Portrait gift crops4:5 photography, the retail standard, instead of letterbox tiles. Drag a real product image onto any of them.
Numbers behaveTabular figures so prices align down a column, champagne rather than school-bus yellow on the dark rail.
